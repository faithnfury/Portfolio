How an engagement runs

Scoped to the operation, not the calendar
01

Assess the operation

Line audits, OEE and first-principles analysis, and data pulls (SPC, Cp/Cpk, FMEA) to find where the operation is actually losing throughput, yield, or capacity — not just where it's assumed to be.

02

Redesign & build the case

Trade studies across manual, semi-automated, and fully automated options, modeled in CAD and simulation, with a cost-impact business case built for leadership sign-off.

03

Stabilize & hand off

SOPs, containment procedures, and control charts that keep the operation in statistical control after handoff — with the documentation to train the team running it day to day.
